## Manage

Inside a Swarm you can:

| Action | Note |
|---|---|
| Manage members | add or remove members and change their nickname, prompt and settings; the work tree cannot change |
| Clear transcript | clears the transcript and member sessions, keeping the Swarm and its members |
| Delete swarm | deletes the Swarm and its transcript |

## Templates

**Save as template** downloads the Swarm as a JSON file with its name, member list and member configuration; it **omits** the machine-specific work tree, the id and the transcript.

In the Swarm list, **Import template** creates a Swarm from such a file. Use it to reuse the same division of labour across projects or machines.
