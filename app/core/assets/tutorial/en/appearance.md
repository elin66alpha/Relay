All in Settings, and they affect only this device.

## Appearance

- **Theme**: System, Light or Dark. The terminal's colours follow it too.
- **Font size**: 85% to 130% for text across the app; **Reset font size** returns to 100%.
- **Wallpaper**: see [Wallpaper](wallpaper.md).

## Language

English or 中文. Switching changes the interface and this tutorial.

## Other settings

- [Machine credentials](import-credential.md) (saved machines, **Add machine**, and **Test current machine**) are at the top of Settings.
- The notification switches: see [Notifications](notifications.md).
- **About** holds the version and [Check for updates](app-update.md).
